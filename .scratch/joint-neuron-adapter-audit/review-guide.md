# Adversarial review guide — draft v2

Review the spec, both itemized review responses, the proposed candidate plan and each ticket before implementation. The files are `needs-triage`; the plan is explicitly unapproved and is not permission to execute or spend on a Pod.

1. **Scientific contracts:** Check the distinction between post-token activation, native writes, rank-one read, physical vector write and activation differences. Check gauge orientation, source/style confounding, same-batch pairing, interpretation of noise and the absence of behavioral/causal claims.
2. **Selection and inference:** Check primary D is |mean paired change| and relative D is secondary; ensure independent discovery lists, frozen held-out evaluation, explicit activity-matched-null eligible populations, conditional-exchangeability assumptions, actual pooled estimand and undefined/underfilled cases. Verify null intervals are not presented as confidence intervals.
3. **Qualification:** Check every selected live adapter against saved keys and factors, active routing and an independently loaded base; examine zero-update and sub-resolution edge cases. Ensure proposed FP32/TF32-off and optional FP64/BF16 comparisons use independently measured statistics and predeclared thresholds; a base-only noise floor is not a bound on every adapted run.
4. **Operational boundaries:** Inspect the actual launch/worker process for interpreter, cache and offline environment; separately test online HF credentials and write/readback; check output/temp storage, observed throughput and progress. Consider CLI versions, mount, clock, serialization, locking semantics, runtime changes, SSH disconnects, stale state, failure recovery and budgeting.
5. **Artifact scale and restore:** Check dense-block formulas, token-level output counts, available temporary space and network volume quota. Inspect hash-cache trust assumptions, snapshot consistency, omitted dependencies and clean-root restore. A verified partial export must remain partial.
6. **Ticket graph:** 01 → 02 → 03a → 07 → 03b → 04 → 06; 05 is independently implementable and fixture-testable after 03a and also blocks 06. Ticket 07 must work before qualification or production and after failure; it is not blocked by the report.
7. **Open choices:** Scrutinize the exact overlap-null statistic and pooling, numeric and floor definitions, data and leakage policy, background filter, physical precision, false-positive/false-negative tests, head scope, and behavior if source evidence or an arm is missing. Do not turn a proposed value into retrospective approval.
8. **Round 2 specifics:** Check the missing offline adapter config hash rather than substituting its original training config; calibration-set disjointness; Qwen boundaries at actual token granularity; the 99th-percentile FP64 calibration floor's limitations; a discrete-null test against exact enumeration; and the candidate plan's unresolved fields. The user will choose the hard wall-time cap after the benchmark.
