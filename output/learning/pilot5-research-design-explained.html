Can we read what
fine-tuning left behind?

Give two adapted models the same unrelated text. Subtract the original model’s internal activations. Then ask whether the direction of that change reveals the task the adapters were trained on.

Pilot 5 moves from measuring how much the internals changed to testing what can be read from those changes. It reuses the existing adapters and introduces a new measurement corpus, qualified readouts, controls and statistical assessment.

01 / Motivation & research questions

Behaviour tells us what changed.
Can internal differences tell us what was learned?

Earlier pilots supply trained adapters, behavioural evidence and measurements of internal contributions and block-output changes. Those measurements can locate or quantify a shift. A vector’s magnitude or cosine does not directly tell us whether its direction contains recognisable arithmetic-task content.

A

The behavioural question

Does the model solve GSM8K questions? Does performance change after SFT or GRPO? This requires generated answers and an answer scorer.

Existing behavioural results are context. Pilot 5 does not regenerate them, and this experiment does not add a new training run.

B

The readable-trace question

When the model reads unrelated web text, does the adapter consistently move its internal representation along a direction that a lens reads as task-related?

This requires paired activations, a declared vector, a qualified lens and a defensible assessment—not an answer-generation score.

Secondary questions: Where across depth and content position is the trace strongest? How stable is it as the number of documents changes? Do two lenses agree? Does a result depend on the reference vocabulary or corpus stratum? Intermediate checkpoints and optional formatting are additional scope choices, not a license to search for the best primary result. [S2]

Why compare gold-SFT and GRPO?

SFT: learn from a supplied solution

The supervised arm learns to assign probability to the provided gold-answer tokens. Its training signal directly contains the wording, reasoning text and answer conventions of those examples.

A plausible hypothesis is that this leaves an accessible lexical or formatting signature. That is a motivation to test, not an observed Pilot 5 finding.

GRPO: learn from scored samples

The policy produces groups of answers, receives correctness rewards and is updated using relative within-group signal. Its sampled text and learning trajectory differ from copying the gold solutions.

A trace could be weaker, stronger or expressed differently. The same rank-one constraint and model identity do not imply the same activation geometry.

For the broader selective-fine-tuning project, this is an auditability question: can we identify what an adaptation is related to by examining its internal effects on unrelated inputs? Comparing behavioural context, geometric changes and readable traces provides three views of the same saved adapters, while leaving causal attribution to further experiments.

A controlled comparison of these saved adapters, not a universal ranking of SFT and GRPO. Both arms share the model and training cohort, but their training objectives and trajectories differ. One adapter run per method does not estimate variation across training seeds or isolate every effect of the training algorithm.
02 / Objects before operations

Keep weights, activations, differences
and readouts distinct.

A fine-tuned model is the base model plus an adapter. A forward pass creates activations. Subtracting paired activations creates a difference vector. A lens turns that vector into vocabulary scores. These objects answer different questions.

Rank-1 weights ≠ a rank-1 trace

ΔW = s · B AA has shape 1 × input width; B has shape output width × 1. Here r = 1 and the saved protocol uses scaling 1.

This constrains each adapted linear projection’s weight update. Across many projections, nonlinearities, residual paths and inputs, the resulting activation changes need not all lie in one global direction.

Direct contribution ≠ block-output change

direct write: ΔW · x
block shift: hadapted − hbase

Pilot 5 reads the second quantity. It includes a module’s own update and changes propagated from earlier computation. It is not a decomposition of which LoRA projection caused each token in the readout.

03 / From unrelated text to an average trace

Hold the input fixed.
Change only the adapter condition.

The corpus is FineWeb text rather than GSM8K questions. A readable arithmetic-related direction on this unrelated input would suggest that the adapter leaves an accessible training-related signature beyond explicitly being asked to solve a math problem. Raw input is also out of the training chat format; a weak raw trace could reflect that context mismatch.

Paired capture on one document

No text generation

The disabled-adapter reference can be shared across compatible adapted conditions in the same frozen batch. All 29 hooks observe the same forward pass; 29 sites do not mean 29 separate model runs.

δd,ℓ,pa = hd,ℓ,pa − hd,ℓ,pbase
mda = ⅕ ∑p=0…4 δd,14,pa
δ̄a = ¹⁄₂₀₀ ∑d=1…200 mdad = document; ℓ = decoder block; p = content-relative position; a = SFT-64 or GRPO-64. Average positions within each document, then average documents. The fixed primary is raw / block 14 / step 64 / pooled 0–4.

Why average?

Individual activations contain the current document’s content. Pairing removes the shared base representation, and averaging asks whether a consistent adaptation-induced direction remains across different documents.

Averaging does not guarantee task content. Large shifts can cancel, while a small consistent direction can survive. Norm, coherence and SNR provide context for the decoded result.

Why preserve positions?

Pooling all 128 positions at capture time could erase an early-position signal. Per-position sums retain the ability to inspect depth and position without recapturing the model.

The main test is already fixed to pooled positions 0–4. Individual positions, late windows and other sites are secondary, even if they look stronger afterward.

Same average operation; different kinds of signal

Illustrative vectors—not measured results

A common direction is visible in the mean. Whether it is task-related remains a lens-and-control question.

A weak cohort mean does not imply that individual documents or other representations contain no task information.

Content position zero is not automatically a BOS token. The raw tokenizer adds no special token. The corpus records actual offsets, excluded special positions and the exact content-to-input map. In an optional assistant-form extension, content position zero would occur after the prompt/header, at a different absolute input position. [S3]
04 / Reading a direction

The lens produces the tokens.
The scorer assesses them afterward.

An activation vector has 1,536 coordinates with no immediately readable labels. A lens maps that vector into vocabulary scores. Sorting those scores produces the direct experimental token list. The model is not generating a response here, and an LLM grader does not create the lens output.

Logit lens

Use the model’s output coordinates.

scores(v) = WU · RMSNormγ,ε(v)

The final norm’s gain weights γ and the unembedding WU define the mapping. Omitting gain can change rankings. A positive rescaling of a nonzero vector preserves ranking under this mapping; magnitude is still recorded separately.

Earlier-layer vectors may not already be expressed in the final output representation. That is an assumption to qualify and report, not a reason to treat every attractive token as literal internal text.

Community Jacobian lens

Use a separately calibrated readout.

The proposed community artifact supplies an alternative lens for the same model family. Its exact mapping, source sites, target representation, norm convention, dimensions and provenance must be inspected before use.

Ticket 05 must establish compatibility. A filename mentioning Qwen is insufficient. If it fails, save the evidence and ask about a separately approved fallback; do not quietly train a new lens or drop half the primary family.

The precise operator is deliberately not asserted here before that inspection. A second readout gives a robustness comparison, not an independent causal proof.

1
Rank raw token scores deterministically

Primary assessment ranks scores in descending order for the positive vector direction. This is a direction convention, not a strict score > 0 filter. Break ties by token ID. Negative lists can be retained as labelled secondary evidence.

2
Normalize decoded strings

NFKC(decode(single token ID)).strip().lower() merges whitespace/case variants such as total and Total. Exclude added/special IDs, empty strings and replacement-character fragments.

3
Keep up to 20 unique valid strings

Scan at most the top 1,000 raw IDs. Do not count five token IDs representing the same normalized word as five different hits. A short list uses its actual valid count; zero valid strings is undefined.

A zero vector has no meaningful ranked interpretation. Step-zero adapters should yield δ = 0. All tied vocabulary scores from that vector are not a “trace.” The implementation records the zero/undefined state rather than reporting whichever tokens a tie-break happens to place first. [S2]
05 / Turning a readable list into assessable evidence

Define relevance before looking.
Then compare it with the right control.

“This looks mathematical” is useful for inspection, but vulnerable to selective interpretation. The approved analysis creates task references independently of learned readouts, freezes the procedure, and retains both the token lists and quantitative scores. The later v1.1 amendment supersedes earlier token-ID/add-one proposals. [S2]

Follow the assessment pipeline

Choose a step

Three text sources define three references

  • Primary: the exact 512 SFT training questions.
  • Secondary: their 512 gold solutions.
  • Secondary: all 4,096 accepted GRPO completions, including incorrect ones.

Questions emphasize task wording; gold and generated solutions can emphasize answer conventions and formatting. This distinction prevents a “####” trace from automatically being described as broad task understanding.

Compare against ordinary-text occurrence counts

Scan the pinned first 10,000 FineWeb documents, exclude measurement documents and duplicate identities, drop short candidates, shuffle with the fixed local seed, cap each document at 1,024 tokens and select exactly 2,000,000 raw token IDs.

Informative-prior log odds ranks normalized strings enriched in task text. Require task count ≥ 5 and positive z; keep up to 200. Prior mass α₀ = 1,000; sensitivity variants 100 and 10,000. Review the top 30 before accepting the reference freeze.

Two corpora, two jobs.200 × 128 content positions elicit activation changes on the GPU. The separate 2M-token background builds reference vocabulary counts on CPU. The model is not run over that background in Ticket 03.

A small, deliberately mechanical score

overlap = valid top strings in the reference / number of valid top strings

Illustration only: seven highlighted strings out of twenty valid strings yield 0.35. These are invented teaching tokens, not a Pilot 5 readout or frozen vocabulary.

totaltheapplesroadeachafterbluecosttreedollarswindowevenremaininghomethissumwouldacrossquietstory

Word versus number/format overlap and soft-rank correlation provide supporting views. None makes reference membership a ground-truth semantic label: a synonym can miss the vocabulary, and a format token can hit it.

Could an unrelated direction look this relevant through this lens?

Draw 1,000 isotropic Gaussian vectors, normalize each to unit length, pass them through the same qualified lens and use the same reference and list rules. The null controls for what the readout and vocabulary make easy to see from generic directions.

p = (1 + number of null scores ≥ observed) / 1,001

Toy arithmetic: if five of 1,000 null scores are at least as large as the observation, p = 6/1,001. This is not an observed result. Unit normalization avoids tying null construction to a learned vector’s magnitude. A secondary max-over-five statistic must use the same maximum in its null.

The optional activation-shaped null uses qualified base covariance. Its inputs are collected during capture; it is unavailable if those moments are absent. Real null score arrays are generated after lens qualification and before learned-score publication.

Resample documents, then rebuild the vector and readout.

A bootstrap draw samples document IDs with replacement. It keeps all five primary positions and both arms together for each selected document. Recompute the mean vector, apply the lens again and score the resulting list. Do not bootstrap isolated tokens as if they were independent documents.

1,000primary-score draws2,000paired SFT–GRPO draws2,000within-stratum drawsSame IDsboth arms in each paired draw

The top-token operation is nonlinear and can change abruptly near ties. Resampling saved scalar scores would not reproduce the uncertainty of reconstructing the mean-vector readout.

Three claims need three analyses

C1 and C2 form four primary tests: {SFT, GRPO} × {logit, Jacobian lens}, using the question reference and fixed primary vector. Holm correction at α = 0.05 accounts for that declared family. C3 compares the methods directly with a paired document bootstrap and a two-lens family.

C3 has a specific inferential limitation.The approved implementation computes the declared two-sided bootstrap-tail statistic and percentile interval. Tiny formula/calibration tests do not establish its nominal false-positive rate for the eventual discontinuous ranking statistic. The report must retain that caveat; a stronger inferential claim may require a separately reviewed calibrated procedure.

How much text is enough?

N-curves use N = 25, 50, 100, 200, with 200 subsets per N. They examine stability within the fixed corpus. At N = 200, every subset contains the full cohort; those repeats do not create 200 independent cohorts.

SNR = ‖mean document vector‖ / SESE = √[Σd ‖md − m̄‖² / (N(N−1))]. Undefined for N < 2 or zero SE. Higher SNR is not proof of greater semantic information.

Why keep the 150 / 50 distinction?

Legacy documents connect to earlier pilots; the added 50 provide a declared stratum check. Compare each with its own uncertainty, then compare new-50 with 1,000 size-matched legacy-50 subsets.

“New” means newly added to this measurement cohort. It does not mean absent from pretraining or unseen by every researcher. Differences are descriptive, not a randomized effect of exposure.

What does optional blinded grading add?

Automatic overlap checks a frozen lexicon. A separate semantic supplement asks either whether tokens are relevant to a disclosed task, or whether a grader can identify the task among eight candidates. These are different questions. Naming happens first in a fresh isolated session. The approved inventory has 106 lists per mode: 36 difference lists, 30 base/adapted baseline lists and 40 null lists. Opaque IDs hide arm/lens/position metadata, private keys are separate, disagreement must be settled before condition joins, and agreement/κ are reported. No API calls are automatic. A pending supplement does not block the automatic report.

A6 → base moments

The improved design asks capture to accumulate Σh and Σhhᵀ in FP64. Production count is 200 × 127 = 25,400. Position zero is excluded by the declared covariance rule.

μ = Σh / n
Σ = (Σhhᵀ − n μμᵀ) / (n − 1)

At width 1,536, the FP64 second-moment matrix alone is 18 MiB. Its accumulation and transfer costs belong in the profile. Missing evidence means the secondary null is unavailable.

A7 → separate positions survive

Each document’s five early-position deltas must survive, not just its five-position mean. The same retained objects support the primary pooled bootstrap and the secondary maximum over individual positions.

The Ticket 04 reducer and independent tests check both these A6/A7 requirements. Ticket 07 must implement their full production writer and durable release; a tested reducer is not a completed production capture.

Arithmetic precision and storage precision are separate decisions. Model activations are FP32; sums and primary pooled evidence are FP64. FP16 retained token shards are a proposal awaiting measured conversion/ranking/bootstrap sensitivity. FP64 accumulation does not make an FP16-resampled analysis exact.

08 / The ticket architecture

Each slice delivers a usable boundary.
The experiment comes from joining them.

A ticket is a vertical capability with inputs, an operation, saved outputs and a verification path. It extends the same shared notebook. CPU algorithms can be implemented and tested before real captured data exist; that does not make the corresponding scientific result executed or validated.

The dependency graph

Solid = required · dashed = optional supplement / extension

Green: real bootstrap/corpus gates validated. Amber: implementation ready but target execution/review pending. Gray: later capabilities. This is a status snapshot, not a statement that the full pipeline can run today. [S5]

Why Ticket 03 looked larger than one notebook stepThe immediate runnable slice is reference preparation and review. Its approved scope also implemented reusable scoring, null, bootstrap, robustness and blinded-package algorithms on synthetic data. Tickets 08–10 will integrate and execute those algorithms on qualified real evidence; they are not supposed to rebuild that math.

What a seal means

Plan identityThe exact sources, input order, settings and code identity that a result belongs to.
Payload integrityHashes confirm that saved arrays and evidence match the completed unit.
Explicit completionA status message alone is not proof. Required files and seals must verify.
Scoped trustChecksums detect accidental changes. They do not protect against someone deliberately rewriting the whole history.
Resume means “reuse valid units,” not “trust that the last cell looked finished.”The Ticket 04 unit is a condition: base, SFT-zero or GRPO-zero on four fixed documents. A restart verifies those seals and computes only missing conditions. An interrupted unsealed condition may repeat. Future production uses its own frozen batch/condition units; do not assume this bounded diagnostic is the full production recovery implementation.

Three identities to keep separate

Source identity: which model, adapter and inputs?

Numerical settings: precision, attention, batch membership and position mapping?

Runtime: which GPU, libraries and code release produced the evidence?

A new GPU does not erase valid training results. It can require a new diagnostic or an explicitly qualified bridge for numerical evidence. Never silently merge incompatible executions.

Three kinds of checking

Restore: recover saved pointers without model work.

Integrity verification: validate the relevant saved payloads and parents.

Numerical qualification: establish that the instrument measures the intended quantity under declared settings.

These checks have different purposes. Repeating a full optimizer-state audit just to restore a notebook variable would not improve capture mathematics.

What is the runtime estimate today?

There is no verified end-to-end Pilot 5 runtime yet. The four-document diagnostic is bounded and much smaller than production, but setup, first model download, whole-base hash reads and Drive saves can dominate it. The latest implementation session’s full regression took about 17 minutes; that is developer CPU test time, not a model-run benchmark. Production timing is deliberately deferred to measured Ticket 06 profiling. Use the workload explorer only for arithmetic scenarios, not as a priced ETA.

What happens after a restart or a different GPU?

Restore the saved selection from Drive in a fresh process. Sealed units are reused only under the appropriate source/runtime/code identity; missing units are computed. A different GPU can require a separately named diagnostic, rather than rebuilding SFT/GRPO training or deleting earlier evidence. For Ticket 04, change the diagnostic name in preparation once if needed; subsequent cells restore that saved name. Broader production bridges remain an explicitly reviewed capability, not a silent override.

Current diagnostic storage is deliberately simple and uncompressed: approximately 87 MiB of FP32 base activations, two 174 MiB FP64 zero-difference units and an 18 MiB moment matrix at width 1,536/128 positions—about 453 MiB before metadata. This is distinct from the proposed production FP16 shard format. [S6]

Final research outputs

  • Primary C1/C2 table: overlap, null percentile/p, correction, uncertainty and SNR.
  • Paired C3 contrast per lens with its interval and inferential caveat.
  • Direct token records, positive/negative signs and control identities.
  • Secondary depth/position/reference plots, N-curves and stratum checks.
  • Optional semantic supplement with agreement and disclosure.
  • Verified configs, artifact inventory, numerical coverage and limitations.

Why the mechanistic comparison is useful

Combining behaviour with matched-input internal measurements can reveal whether similarly performing adapters leave different accessible signatures, or whether different performance accompanies similar mean traces.

This can motivate targeted interventions or finer decomposition. It is a useful intermediate measurement, not by itself a novelty claim or a demonstrated circuit explanation. Establishing novelty would require a separate literature comparison.

The full chain of reasoning
Verify the trained objects → supply identical unrelated inputs → measure a declared change → decode it with qualified readouts → assess against independently defined references and controls → report the claim at the level the evidence supports.
