02 / First principles

Can internal changes tell us the training task?

Imagine two people reading the same unrelated paragraph. One has recently studied school mathematics. Does the difference in their internal processing carry a recognizable mathematical signature?

Here, we compare the same instruction-tuned Qwen model with and without an adapter. The shared input is fixed FineWeb text. We subtract paired hidden activations and use a lens to express their average difference as ranked vocabulary tokens.

The approved primary vector: use block index 14 at checkpoint 64; average positions 0–4 within each document, then average across the 200 documents. That yields one vector per learned arm. It is one pooled window, not five independent primary tests.

Capture is planned at 29 sites: embedding output plus outputs of decoder blocks 0–27, before the final model norm. These are not 29 decoder blocks. Raw inputs have no chat template; content position 0 must be mapped from actual tokenization rather than assumed to be a BOS token.

07 / What questions did I mean?

The vocabulary is a ruler we must choose before measuring.

The lens directly produces ranked tokens. The vocabulary is a separate assessment: a fixed set of 200 GSM8K-enriched token IDs. Automatic relevance is the fraction of the top 20 readout tokens belonging to that set. It does not generate the lens output or replace reading it.

None of these unanswered choices has been silently approved.
The review drafts contain empty fields. Ticket 03 is not complete, no rules have been frozen, and no learned token lists have been exposed by this code.
Why save who saw earlier outputs?

Suppose we inspect a learned token list, then select a vocabulary that makes that list look especially task-related. The measurement can still be useful, but the assessment was influenced by the outcome. The history should say so. Scalar Pilot 4 accuracy or activation plots are different exposure from Pilot 5 learned lens lists. Unknown history remains explicitly unknown. Recording exposure is not proof of blinding.

Why not just look at the token lists?

We should retain and display them once the review gate is satisfied. The automatic score adds a reproducible comparison against controls. Optional blinded grading adds a separate semantic assessment. These answer related but different questions; neither can make an unqualified lens or corrupted activation run scientifically valid.

Why not bootstrap every token at every layer?

A mean vector alone does not retain the variation across individual documents. Whole-document bootstrap requires per-example evidence. We plan to keep pooled primary-window evidence at all sites and individual-token evidence at six selected blocks. The new guard rejects requests outside that contract before promising an analysis. That protects us from discovering an expensive storage/capture dependency late.

08 / What changes after the Pilot 4 operational problems?

Separate scientific identity from session convenience.

Fresh kernels restore paths

Saved selections and manifests restore state. Setup does not retrain old adapters or regenerate earlier responses.

Verification has a scope

Corpus preparation verifies the input seal and input payloads. It does not repeatedly hash optimizer checkpoints it never uses. Capture must still qualify its own sources.

GPU release has a boundary

Planned capture stages must seal and verify durable evidence before advising release. A GPU/runtime change must be assessed explicitly, not treated as automatic corruption or silently ignored.

These foundations reduce specific failure modes. They do not establish that future GPU capture, the community lens or cross-runtime comparisons already work. Those are bounded qualification tickets with their own evidence and budget gates.
